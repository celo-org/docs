This page lists providers for adding wallets to an app on Celo. If you hold CELO and want to choose a wallet to use, see [Wallets for users](/learn/wallets); to list your Mini App inside MiniPay, see [Build for MiniPay](/build-on-celo/build-on-minipay/overview).

<Note>
  **Fee abstraction depends on the wallet.** Paying gas in stablecoins requires the wallet to build a CIP-64 transaction. A wallet without that support drops the app's `feeCurrency` and charges gas in CELO. See [which wallets support it](/build-on-celo/fee-abstraction/overview#wallet-support-for-cip-64).
</Note>

## Wallet providers

The list is in no particular order and is not a recommendation.

---

### [Privy](https://www.privy.io/)

Whitelabel wallets, transaction signing and onchain infrastructure through one API.

- Homepage: [privy.io](https://www.privy.io/)
- [Source Code](https://github.com/privy-io)

---

### [Openfort](https://www.openfort.io/)

Non-custodial embedded wallets with social login, backend (server) wallets, and account abstraction with paymasters for gasless transactions and paying fees in stablecoins.

- Homepage: [openfort.io](https://www.openfort.io/)
- [Docs](https://www.openfort.io/docs)
- [Source Code](https://github.com/openfort-xyz)

---

### [Alchemy Smart Wallets](https://www.alchemy.com/smart-wallets)

Smart wallet infrastructure from Alchemy.

- Homepage: [alchemy.com](https://www.alchemy.com)
- [Docs](https://www.alchemy.com/smart-wallets)

---

### [thirdweb](https://thirdweb.com/wallets)

Self-custodial embedded wallet infrastructure.

- Homepage: [thirdweb.com](https://thirdweb.com/)
- [Docs](https://portal.thirdweb.com/wallets)

---

### [Reown](https://reown.com/)

Reown, formerly WalletConnect Inc., maintains WalletConnect, which connects apps to the wallets users already have.

- Homepage: [reown.com](https://reown.com/)
- [Reown on Celo](/tooling/libraries-sdks/reown)
- [Source Code](https://github.com/WalletConnect)

---

### [Dynamic](https://www.dynamic.xyz/)

Wallet connection and authentication platform with built-in Celo support, covering multiple wallets, network switching and multi-wallet linking.

- Homepage: [dynamic.xyz](https://www.dynamic.xyz/)
- [Dynamic on Celo](/tooling/libraries-sdks/dynamic)

---

### [BlockRadar](https://www.blockradar.co/)

Wallet as a service for fintechs handling stablecoin transactions.

- Homepage: [www.blockradar.co](https://www.blockradar.co/)
- [Docs](https://docs.blockradar.co/introduction)
- [Source Code](https://github.com/iamnotstatic/multichain-crypto-wallet)

---

### [Para](https://www.getpara.com/)

Wallet and authentication suite for crypto applications.

- Homepage: [getpara.com](https://www.getpara.com/)
- [Docs](https://docs.getpara.com/)
- [Source Code](https://github.com/getpara)

---

### [Safe Wallet](https://app.safe.global/welcome)

Smart account and multisig wallet platform. To sign Safe transactions with a Ledger over WalletConnect, see the [EIP-712 signing workaround](/tooling/wallets/ledger/eip712-workaround).

- Homepage: [app.safe.global](https://app.safe.global/welcome)
- [Docs](https://docs.safe.global/home/what-is-safe)
- [Source Code](https://github.com/safe-global/safe-wallet-monorepo)

---

### [Zerion](https://zerion.io/)

Zerion's developer API for building apps and wallets.

- Homepage: [zerion.io](https://zerion.io/)
- [API reference](https://developers.zerion.io/reference/getting-started)

---

### [Portal](https://portalhq.io/)

MPC wallet provider: embedded MPC wallets for users, and an SDK for the web3 functionality of an app.

- Homepage: [portalhq.io](https://portalhq.io/)
- [Portal on Celo](/tooling/libraries-sdks/portal)
- Platforms: [iOS](https://docs.portalhq.io/guides/ios), [Android](https://docs.portalhq.io/guides/android), [Browser](https://docs.portalhq.io/guides/web), [Flutter](https://docs.portalhq.io/resources/flutter), [API](https://docs.portalhq.io/guides/enclave-mpc-api), [React Native](https://docs.portalhq.io/guides/react-native)
- [Documentation](https://docs.portalhq.io/)

---

### [JAW](https://jaw.id/)

Smart account infrastructure with passkey-secured accounts, ENS identity, gas sponsorship, and programmable permissions for subscriptions and delegated agent execution, through a TypeScript SDK.

- Homepage: [jaw.id](https://jaw.id/)
- [JAW on Celo](/tooling/libraries-sdks/jaw)
- [Docs](https://docs.jaw.id/)
- [Source Code](https://github.com/JustaName-id/jaw-mono)
- [Dashboard](https://dashboard.jaw.id/)
