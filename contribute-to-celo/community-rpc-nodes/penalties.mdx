## Overview

The Celo community has established penalties for Community RPC providers who fail to maintain their RPC nodes. These penalties ensure providers maintain reliable and consistent service to the network.

## How it works

The per-epoch USDm payment to Community RPC providers is 0, set by governance in [CGP-0218](https://github.com/celo-org/governance/blob/main/CGPs/cgp-0218.md). Scores still count: a group's score scales the CELO rewards paid to voters for that group. Scores are monitored off-chain by the Score Management Committee, an independent working group running custom software based on [Vido by Atalma](https://dev.vido.atalma.io/celo/rpc). This committee operates a Safe Multisig with permissions to manage the on-chain `ScoreManager.sol` smart contract.

Weekly, the committee collates measurements and averages scores for each provider. Scores below 1 are updated and apply to the following week's rewards.

**Running a Community RPC is mandatory for elected providers.** Insufficient uptime results in slashing - a portion of locked CELO is forfeited.

### The Score Management Committee

The Committee controls a multisig with governance-granted powers to call functions on the `ScoreManager` and `GovernanceSlasher` contracts. Each member receives $2k USDm monthly for operational expenses.

**Responsibilities:**

- Running open-source and verifiable monitoring infrastructure for uptime tracking
- Performance evaluation, collaboration, and multisig operations
- Transparent communication

### Metrics

Rewards are calculated automatically after each epoch from `ScoreManager` contract scores. Voter rewards in CELO are distributed automatically; the provider payment is 0.

Weekly score breakdown:

| RPC Uptime | Score |
|------------|-------|
| 80% - 100% | 1.00  |
| 60% - 79%  | 0.80  |
| 40% - 59%  | 0.60  |
| 20% - 39%  | 0.40  |
| 0% - 19%   | 0 and Slash |

Providers with uptime below 20% for 7 days are slashed.
