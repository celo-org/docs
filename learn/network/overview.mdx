### Tokens and fee currencies on Celo Mainnet

Use the **token address** to transfer or approve a token. Use the **feeCurrency address** as the `feeCurrency` field of a transaction to pay gas in that token. The two differ for USDC and USDT: they use 6 decimals, and gas is priced in 18, so they are allowlisted through an adapter contract. To pay gas in CELO, leave `feeCurrency` out.

| Token | Decimals | Token address (Celo Mainnet, 42220) | feeCurrency address (Celo Mainnet, 42220) |
| ----- | -------- | ----------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| CELO | 18 | [`0x471EcE3750Da237f93B8E339c536989b8978a438`](https://celo.blockscout.com/address/0x471EcE3750Da237f93B8E339c536989b8978a438) | Not needed: leave `feeCurrency` out |
| USDm | 18 | [`0x765DE816845861e75A25fCA122bb6898B8B1282a`](https://celo.blockscout.com/address/0x765DE816845861e75A25fCA122bb6898B8B1282a) | [`0x765DE816845861e75A25fCA122bb6898B8B1282a`](https://celo.blockscout.com/address/0x765DE816845861e75A25fCA122bb6898B8B1282a) (same as the token) |
| USDC | 6 | [`0xcebA9300f2b948710d2653dD7B07f33A8B32118C`](https://celo.blockscout.com/address/0xcebA9300f2b948710d2653dD7B07f33A8B32118C) | [`0x2F25deB3848C207fc8E0c34035B3Ba7fC157602B`](https://celo.blockscout.com/address/0x2F25deB3848C207fc8E0c34035B3Ba7fC157602B) (adapter) |
| USDT | 6 | [`0x48065fbBE25f71C9282ddf5e1cD6D6A887483D5e`](https://celo.blockscout.com/address/0x48065fbBE25f71C9282ddf5e1cD6D6A887483D5e) | [`0x0E2A3e05bc9A16F5292A6170456A710cb89C6f72`](https://celo.blockscout.com/address/0x0E2A3e05bc9A16F5292A6170456A710cb89C6f72) (adapter) |

The fee-currency allowlist lives in the `FeeCurrencyDirectory` contract, [`0x15F344b9E6c3Cb6F0376A36A64928b13F62C6276`](https://celo.blockscout.com/address/0x15F344b9E6c3Cb6F0376A36A64928b13F62C6276) on Celo Mainnet. Every allowlisted token is on the [fee currency contracts](/build/tools/contracts/fee-currencies) page, and every stablecoin on [stablecoin contracts](/build/tools/contracts/stablecoin-contracts).

The Celo Sepolia Testnet is designed for testing and experimentation by developers. Its tokens hold no real world economic value. The testnet software will be upgraded on a regular basis.
