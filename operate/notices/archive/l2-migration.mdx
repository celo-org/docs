---
title: "Celo L2 Migration"
description: Block heights and dates at which Celo mainnet, Baklava and Alfajores migrated from a Layer 1 to an Ethereum Layer 2
---

<Note>
* Mainnet has been migrated on block **31056500**, March 26, 2025, 3:00 AM UTC.
* The Baklava testnet has been migrated on block **28308600**, February 20, 2025.
* The Alfajores testnet has been migrated on block **26384000**, September 26, 2024.
</Note>

The `celo-migrate` tool produced `op-geth` datadirs only, and `op-reth` cannot use them. To run a Celo L2 node, follow [Running a Celo Node](/operate/operators/run-node).
