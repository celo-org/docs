This page is for app and agent developers building lending, swaps, FX, savings or yield products on Celo. It lists the protocols that are live on Celo mainnet and where their documentation and addresses live.

Stablecoins are the unit of account on Celo. Dollar stablecoins ([USA₮](/build/build-with-usat), USDC, USD₮) and [local stablecoins](/build/build-with-local-stablecoin) in more than 20 currencies share one chain, so a protocol can price, lend and settle in the currency its users earn in. Users and agents pay gas in those same stablecoins through [fee abstraction](/build/fee-abstraction/overview), and a typical transaction costs below $0.01. Addresses for every stablecoin are on [Stablecoin contracts](/build/tools/contracts/stablecoin-contracts).

<Note>
  Partner contract addresses are not listed here. Each entry links to the provider's own documentation, which is the canonical source for addresses.
</Note>

## Lending and borrowing

- **[Aave v3](https://aave.com/)** - Variable-rate lending and borrowing markets on Celo, with Chainlink price feeds.
- **[Morpho](https://morpho.org/)** - Isolated lending markets and curated vaults. Any address can create a market; vaults on Celo are curated through [Feather](https://app.feather.zone/celo/earn).
- **[PWN](https://pwn.xyz/)** - Fixed-rate, fixed-term loans against any token.

## Exchanges

For where to buy and swap CELO see [Exchanges](/learn/exchanges). For cross-chain transfers see [Bridges](/build/tools/bridges/bridges) and [Cross-chain messaging](/build/tools/bridges/cross-chain-messaging).

- **[Uniswap v4](https://app.uniswap.org/)** - Concentrated liquidity pools with hooks for custom pool logic. Deployment addresses on Celo are on [Uniswap contracts](/build/tools/contracts/uniswap-contracts).
- **[Textile FX](https://app.textilecredit.com/)** - Onchain FX between local stablecoins and USD₮. Makers quote each corridor from their own price feeds; a trader takes a firm request-for-quote or places a gasless limit order, and the swap settles in one transaction. Live Celo corridors include cNGN, wARS, wBRL, wCOP, wMXN, IDRX and G$ against USD₮. The [Textile docs](https://docs.textilecredit.com/) cover the RFQ API and the [address book](https://docs.textilecredit.com/protocol/address-book.html).
- **[Mento](https://www.mento.org/)** - Onchain swaps between the Mento stablecoins, Celo's onchain FX for local currencies. See [Build with local stablecoins](/build/build-with-local-stablecoin).
- **[Carbon DeFi](https://www.carbondefi.xyz/)** - Onchain limit and range orders with automated strategies.
- **[Ubeswap](https://ubeswap.org/)** - Celo-native exchange built for mobile users.

## DEX aggregators

- **[LI.FI](https://li.fi/)** - Cross-chain swap and bridge routing.
- **[Matcha](https://matcha.xyz/)** - Cross-chain aggregator with limit orders.

## Liquidity incentives

- **[Merkl](https://app.merkl.xyz/)** - Reward campaigns for liquidity providers, paid in stablecoins.
- **[Steer Protocol](https://steer.finance/)** - Automated liquidity strategies for concentrated-liquidity pools.
- **[Ichi](https://www.ichi.org/)** - Single-sided automated liquidity vaults.

## Derivatives

- **[Lynx](https://lynx.finance/)** - Perpetual contracts with leverage.

## Oracles

Price feeds for a lending market, a vault or an FX agent come from an onchain oracle, not from a web API. The [oracles section](/build/tools/oracles) lists every provider on Celo.

- [RedStone](/build/tools/oracles/redstone)
- [Chainlink price feeds](https://docs.chain.link/data-feeds/price-feeds/addresses?network=celo)
- [Supra](/build/tools/oracles/supra)
- [Band Protocol](/build/tools/oracles/band-protocol)

## Transaction decoding

- **[Noves](https://docs.noves.fi/reference/api-overview)** - Human-readable descriptions of DeFi and bridge transactions, pre-sign simulations for wallets, and CELO, USDm and stCELO pricing.

## Agentic DeFi

An AI agent on Celo can quote and settle FX between local stablecoins on Textile, swap on Uniswap, and lend or earn on Aave and Morpho, paying gas in the stablecoin it already holds. [Agentic DeFi on Celo](/build/agents/defi) walks through the loop, the API calls and the guardrails an agent needs.
